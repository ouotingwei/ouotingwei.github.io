## 👨‍💻 About Me
I am now pursuing a Master's in Robotics at **NYCU** under the advisement of Prof. **Kuu-Young Young**. Based in the **Human and Machine Lab**, my current research focuses on computer vision and robotics.

Before pursuing graduate studies, I worked as an Automatic System Development Engineer at **Hong Lang Technology Co., Ltd.** (2023-2024), where I helped modernize traditional footwear manufacturing by developing automated machinery systems for industrial production.

During my undergraduate years, I served as the team leader of the **NYCU iTron Robotic Team** (2020-2021), leading the design, integration, and competition deployment of student-built robotic platforms.
